Fried Chicken

What You Need

  • 1 kg chicken pieces
  • 2 cups buttermilk
  • 2 cups all-purpose flour
  • 1 tsp paprika
  • 1 tsp garlic powder
  • 1 tsp salt
  • 1/2 tsp black pepper
  • Oil for frying

How to Do It

  1. Marinate the chicken in buttermilk for at least 1 hour, preferably overnight.
  2. In a bowl, mix flour, paprika, garlic powder, salt, and black pepper.
  3. Heat oil in a deep frying pan over medium-high heat.
  4. Remove chicken from buttermilk and dredge each piece in the flour mixture, coating evenly.
  5. Fry chicken in batches until golden brown and cooked through, about 12-15 minutes per batch.
  6. Drain on paper towels and serve hot.

Pizza

What You Need

  • 1 pizza base
  • 1/2 cup pizza sauce
  • 1 cup shredded mozzarella cheese
  • Your choice of toppings (pepperoni, bell peppers, onions, olives, etc.)
  • 1 tsp olive oil
  • 1/2 tsp dried oregano

How to Do It

  1. Preheat your oven to 220°C (430°F).
  2. Place the pizza base on a baking tray or pizza stone.
  3. Spread pizza sauce evenly over the base.
  4. Sprinkle mozzarella cheese over the sauce.
  5. Add your desired toppings.
  6. Drizzle olive oil over the pizza and sprinkle with dried oregano.
  7. Bake in the preheated oven for 10-12 minutes, or until the crust is golden and the cheese is bubbly.
  8. Remove from the oven, slice, and serve hot.
